May 2026 – Aug 2026 · Remote

EPR Regulatory Analysis Intern

Circular Action Alliance (CAA)

Multi-state research on how organics mandates interact with Extended Producer Responsibility program design, built so that every claim traces back to its exact statutory source.

  • Researched organics regulations in Maryland, Minnesota and Washington (diversion mandates, collection requirements, disposal bans, food service ware bans and education mandates), each documented with pinpoint statutory citations.
  • Built a multi-state landscape scan comparing statutory obligations, ecomodulation structures and covered materials and entities, showing where mandates constrain, enable or complicate EPR contracting and facility operations.
  • Authored a regulatory analysis on harmonization opportunities between organics legislation, EPR and climate action planning across MD, MN and ME to inform CAA program planning.
  • Extracted quantitative findings from lengthy state needs assessments and public comment records, verifying every figure against the source before it entered the analysis.
  • Designed a library of vetted, reusable AI prompts for core analyst workflows and trained program managers to adopt them. See the workflows →
